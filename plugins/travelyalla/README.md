# TravelYalla

Find cheap flights and hotels with live TravelYalla prices, right inside Claude. TravelYalla covers trips across Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the Gulf and worldwide, and answers in English or Arabic.

## What you can ask

- **Flights:** one-way, round-trip and multi-city. Compare options by price, stops, airline, departure time and baggage allowance.
- **Hotels:** search by city or hotel name, and narrow results by star rating, guest score, price or distance from the city centre. Get a hotel's address, check-in times and facilities.
- **Flexible dates:** find the cheapest day to fly within a date range.
- **Trip plans:** round-trip flights plus a matching hotel, with an estimated total in one currency.

For example:

- "Find the cheapest round-trip flights from Cairo to Dubai next weekend"
- "Show me top-rated hotels in Dubai for 2 adults next weekend"
- "Plan a 5-day trip from Riyadh to Cairo in November"

Every result links to [travelyalla.com](https://travelyalla.com), where you book and pay. The plugin itself only searches: it never takes payment and can't view or change existing bookings.

## What's included

- **MCP server:** `https://mcp.travelyalla.com/mcp`, with read-only tools for destination lookup, flight search, flight details, hotel search and hotel details. No sign-in is needed.
- **Skills:** `get-started`, `find-flights`, `find-hotels`, `flexible-dates` and `plan-trip`, which teach Claude how to collect trip details, run searches and present results with booking links.

## Privacy and support

Search details (route, dates, travellers, nationality) are sent to TravelYalla to fetch live prices. See the [privacy policy](https://travelyalla.com/en-WW/privacy-policy) and [terms](https://travelyalla.com/en-WW/terms-and-conditions). For help, contact [TravelYalla support](https://travelyalla.com/en-WW/contact-us) or info@travelyalla.com.
